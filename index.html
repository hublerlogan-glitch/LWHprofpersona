Domain 2: Communication Style — A Driver by Nature, Perceived as Analytical

The Claim

While my internal communication style is proactive and direct (Driver), my initial outward presentation is perceived by teammates as reserved and methodical (Analytical).

The Evidence (Data)

SPI self-assessment rates as a Driver (+1.0 Proactivity, -4.0 Reactivity), whereas 3 of 4 peers evaluated my external behavior as Analytical (-4.0 Avg Proactivity)—representing a -5.0 point proactivity variance. Qualitative feedback highlighted a pattern of being "quiet initially, then highly engaged once integrated."

Reasoning & Brand Strategy (Intention vs. Impact)

Intention vs. Impact Gap: My intention to maintain harmony led me to listen quietly, but the impact was that teammates missed out on timely, candid critique. To reconcile this, my "Warm & Specific" Brand Strategy implements three actionable habits: (1) Contribute 1 targeted recommendation in every meeting kickoff, (2) Frame execution gaps early with exact deadlines, and (3) Conduct follow-up check-ins to ensure constructive feedback landed effectively.

However, in auditing, partners must balance rapport with firm accountability. Combining high warmth (99.4 environment rating) with direct accountability ensures I can address client discrepancies constructively without sacrificing team cohesion.
